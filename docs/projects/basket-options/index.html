Quantitative Finance · CQF case study

Basket Option Pricing with Deep Learning

A CQF capstone on pricing basket options with up to 100 underlying assets.

Research question

How can neural networks approximate the value of a basket option as the number of underlying assets grows?

Method

I used a forward–backward stochastic differential equation (FBSDE) formulation. The forward process simulates the market variables. The backward process links the option’s value to its payoff at maturity.

I studied two neural architectures: a fully connected multilayer perceptron (FC-MLP) and NAIS-Net. The model set included Black–Scholes, Black–Scholes–Vasicek, and Heston.

Scope and limitations

The capstone studied baskets with up to 100 underlying assets. The pricing formulation depends on the assumed market dynamics, and the learned approximation depends on the training setup.
