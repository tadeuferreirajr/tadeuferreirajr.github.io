Methodology and assumptions

Five-year FCFF forecast from $10B in base revenue. Tax is 25%, depreciation is 3% of revenue, capex is 4%, and incremental net working capital is 10% of the change in revenue. Terminal value uses the Gordon growth formula. Equity value subtracts $3B in net debt from enterprise value and divides by 500M shares.
