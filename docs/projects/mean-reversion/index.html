Methodology and assumptions

A fixed seed generates 756 simulated closes with a drifting trend and a mean-reverting component. The z-score compares each close to the mean and standard deviation of the trailing closes, including the signal day. The simulation opens a hypothetical position at the next close after the z-score falls to or below the threshold and closes it at the next close after the z-score reaches or exceeds zero. Both series start at session 41 after a common warm-up and both include the selected cost per trade side. Any open positions are closed at the final observation. Cash earns zero. The simulation illustrates how the rule behaves under its assumptions.
