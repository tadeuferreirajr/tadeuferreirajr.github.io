Methodology and assumptions

Exact price change for hypothetical 2-year and 10-year par bonds with annual coupons fixed at their baseline yields. The model discounts cash flows at each bond’s new yield to maturity. The steepener applies more of the shock to longer maturities, while the front-end scenario concentrates it at shorter maturities. Negative shocks reverse the selected move. The example isolates the immediate price effect of the change in yields.
